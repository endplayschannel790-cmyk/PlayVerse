ℹ️ About PlayVerse

Your gaming universe

PlayVerse is a gaming-platform prototype for discovering games, saving favorites, creating ideas, and building a profile.

🎨 Our theme

Black backgrounds, electric blue buttons, purple highlights, and glowing accents.

🏆 Verified creator

The Verified rank is intended for eligible, notable game creators. Verification must be reviewed fairly; this prototype does not automatically verify popularity or creator identity.

🔐 Privacy and accounts

This demo uses browser storage. Clearing browser data can erase saved information. Accounts are not shared between devices.
